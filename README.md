# Week-12-Grand-Theft-Vehicles
Plan It:

What is the goal of this website?

To be a 3D driving game set in futuristic Japan, that is inspired by Grand Theft Auto.

Who are you building for, and what do they care about?

Fans of Grand Theft Auto who are waiting for the next game and want something similar, but simpler before it comes out. They need for the game to be in 3D.

Describe how a player should use the game from the moment they visit the site. What should they see first, what should they click, and what should happen next?

Have straight roads where the user can drive. Include jumps on the road and parking areas. Include garages (three different ones) where the user can customize their cars (for example, changing the tires, rims, headlight colors, and opacity of windows).

How should Grand Theft Vehicles work? (3D city, driving, camera, controls)

The player can drive around the city and drive over jumps and enter garages to pay to customize their cars. They will earn income passively while driving. Include an autosave feature.

What words best describe the look and feel that you want?

Futuristic, neon, simple.

Test It:

Fix the walls so they cannot be driven through.

Expand It:

Inside of garage has car on a rotatable stack. Let the user change the color, tint, rim, tire style, spoiler.
